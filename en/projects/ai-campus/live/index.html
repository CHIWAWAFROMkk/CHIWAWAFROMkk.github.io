Live program: survey cleaning and statistics

analysis.py · Python standard library only · running in your browser

This is the study's published statistics program, analysis.py — the very file offered for download under "Run it locally" below — running unchanged in your browser through WebAssembly. Each answer is a point of light that passes the four exclusion gates in the program's order: excluded answers drop into their bin, included ones fill the "included" matrix, and the path of every point comes from the program's own line-by-line trace. Fill in the questionnaire, load the constructed sample, or run a 2,000-answer stress test.
