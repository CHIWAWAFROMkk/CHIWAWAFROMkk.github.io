Routing and review rules (final version)

  • Unreadable, unclear or missing fields → manual review
  • Handwritten forms are the most error-prone → always manual review
  • Verified → entered automatically
  • Every 100 files → all 100 are checked by hand, not sampled

Ran in the company-approved environment; handed over to my mentor
